Privacy Policy

Tapd In · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Tapd In ("we," "our," "us") sends you a weekly briefing built from the email you choose to forward or connect and the text messages you choose to include. We read those messages with an AI service, sort them into topics you set up, and summarize them. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices.

In short

  • We only read email you forward or connect, and texts from contacts you choose.
  • An AI service (Claude, from Anthropic) reads each message to write a short summary.
  • Once a message is summarized, we delete its text. We keep the summary, who it was from, and the subject line.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

1.Information We Collect

Account information. Your email address and password (handled by our authentication provider) and, if you add it, your name.

Messages you forward to us. When you forward an email to your Tapd In address, we receive its content so we can process it:

  • The sender's name and email address, the subject line, and the date it was sent
  • The text of the message (if a message has only formatted content, we convert it to plain text). We do not process or store attachments or photos.

Email from a connected account. If you connect an Outlook or Microsoft 365 account, you approve read-only access on Microsoft's own sign-in screen. We then read new mail in the account's Inbox folder: the sender's name and address, the subject, the date, and the message text. We also look at the date and conversation of your recent Sent Items, never their text, only to tell whether you have already replied to something. We do not read other folders, attachments, or contacts, and we cannot send, delete, or change anything in your mailbox. We store a sign-in token for the connection, encrypted, so we can keep checking for new mail until you disconnect.

Your calendar, only if you turn on meeting briefs. Meeting briefs are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, you approve one extra read-only permission on Microsoft's sign-in screen. When you open your briefing, we read the title, time, and invited attendees (names and email addresses) of your meetings in the next day or so, and match the attendees against people you have already heard from, to show what you know about them before the meeting. We do not read meeting notes, descriptions, attachments, or past meetings, we cannot change your calendar, and we do not store the events: they are used to build that page and discarded. You can turn meeting briefs off in Settings at any time, which stops us reading your calendar.

Text messages from contacts you choose. If you set up texts, you pick the people whose texts count as business. On an iPhone, an automation you create in the Shortcuts app sends us the text, the sender's name or number, and the time, but only for the people you picked. (A Mac helper works the same way.) Texts from anyone else are never read or sent, and texts you send are not collected. We process these texts the same way as forwarded email. Your phone sends them to a private address that contains a long random code, which you can disconnect at any time in Settings.

What we create from them. A short summary of each message, the topic it was sorted into, a flag for whether it looks urgent, a flag for whether the sender looks to be waiting on you for a reply (used for the "Waiting on you" list, which you can clear with Done), and anything the sender promised you, with the date if one was given (used for the "Owed to you" list, which you can clear with Got it).

Your settings. The topics you create and the descriptions you give them, the contacts you allow for texts, and the keys for any Mac you connect (we store only a scrambled version of each key).

Usage information. How many messages were processed and how much processing each used, so we can run and price the service. We also collect basic technical data (such as device type and browser) for security and troubleshooting.

Billing information, if you subscribe to a paid plan. It is handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

2.Messages From Other People

The messages you forward were written by other people, such as your clients, colleagues, or vendors. We process them only on your behalf, to produce your briefing. We do not contact those people, build profiles of them, or use their information for any other purpose. You are responsible for forwarding only messages you are entitled to share.

3.How We Use Information

  • To sort and summarize your messages and to show them to you in the app
  • To email you your weekly briefing and other messages about your account
  • To process payments, if applicable
  • To keep the service secure, working, and improving
  • To respond to your support requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use your messages to train AI models.

4.AI Processing

To write each summary, we send the sender's name, the subject line, and the message text to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI models, through its commercial API. Anthropic processes this content to return the summary. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is not used to train its models, and Anthropic may keep it for a limited time under its own policies. Summaries are generated by an AI and can be wrong, so check anything important against the original message.

5.How Long We Keep Information

  • Message text is held only until the message has been processed, normally within a minute or two. It is then deleted. If a message cannot be processed after a few attempts, its text is deleted and it is marked as failed.
  • Summaries, senders, subject lines, and dates are kept while your account is active, or until you delete them. Choosing Not business on a message deletes it.
  • The sign-in token for a connected email account is kept until you disconnect it, and is deleted when you do.
  • Account information is kept until you ask us to close your account. When we close it, we delete your messages, summaries, and settings.

6.How We Share Information

We share data only with the service providers needed to run Tapd In:

  • Anthropic — AI processing of messages, as described above
  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Vercel — hosting and scheduled jobs
  • Microsoft — provides the read-only access to a mailbox you choose to connect
  • Resend — receiving the email you forward to us and sending your briefing
  • Sentry — error monitoring. We do not intentionally send message content to it.
  • Stripe — payment processing, if you subscribe to a paid plan

These providers process data on our behalf and may not use it for other purposes. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Tapd In, our users, or the public.

7.Connections and Text Messages

Email connections. Connecting Outlook or Microsoft 365 is optional. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, which deletes the stored sign-in so no more mail can be read, and you can also remove Tapd In's access from your Microsoft account. If you prefer, you can forward email to your private address instead of connecting. We do not currently connect to other email providers.

Text messages are included only if you connect a device and choose contacts. Nothing is read from anyone you have not chosen, and the choosing happens on your own device, so other people's texts never leave it. You can remove a contact or disconnect a device at any time in Settings, and it stops sending right away. If we add more connections, they will include only what you choose, and we will update this policy before they are available.

8.Earlier Versions of Tapd In

Earlier versions of Tapd In offered shared project calendars, with invite links, comments, photos, and optional text or email alerts. That feature has been retired and is no longer available. Information from it, such as calendars, notes, uploaded files, and contact details for invited guests, may still be stored until it is deleted. To have it deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

9.Your Choices

  • Delete any message from the app at any time with Not business.
  • Stop forwarding to your address whenever you like. Nothing is read unless you send it.
  • Add or remove your topics in Settings.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, or close your account, by contacting us at the email below.

10.Security

Your forwarding address contains a long random code, and we do not share it. Data is encrypted in transit and is stored with providers that encrypt it at rest. Each account can see only its own messages. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11.Children's Privacy

Tapd In is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

12.Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above, and for changes to how your messages are used we will notify account holders before they take effect.
